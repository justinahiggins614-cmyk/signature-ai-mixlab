# How the Forge works

The Signature AI Mix Lab blends **two parent AIs into one hybrid fusion** —
a Signature recipe, deterministic to the core: **same pair, same hybrid — forever**.

## The two kinds of hybrid

1. **Archived hybrids** — the seeded archive (currently 13,000 records and
   growing by ~1,000 every 2 hours toward the 1,000,000 goal). Stored in the
   archive, searchable, downloadable. Each carries a permanent JAH-MIX-######
   ID that is never reused and never changed.
2. **On-demand hybrids** — any of the remaining ~987,000 numbers computed
   instantly in your browser from the hybrid number alone. Nothing is
   invented: the number *is* the recipe.

## The forge panel

- Enter any number from 1 to 1,000,000 and press **Forge** (the button shows
  your exact number, e.g. "Forge 12").
- The number picks two parents from the parent pool — 260 Signature AIs
  (11 systems, 6 personas, 243 domain specialists) plus 100,000 word-AI
  slots, each word in the Signature Dictionary carrying its own AI.
- **Surprise me** forges a random hybrid drawn from the live seeded archive.

## The seeded drip

Every ~2 hours the Mix Lab seeds the next ~1,000 hybrids into the archive
(latest first), so the searchable archive grows steadily toward 1,000,000.
